The engineering problem

Aerospace health monitoring is not only a prediction problem. A useful prognostics system must respect physical relationships, avoid leakage between engines, expose why a prediction was made and remain consistent when moved from training to deployment.

SubAero was designed around those constraints rather than treating the model as a black box.

Why the model is interpretable

The pipeline uses explicit white-box regression and Explainable Boosting Machines rather than opaque deep networks or black-box ensembles. Polynomial terms, feature contributions and physics-derived quantities can be inspected by an engineer.

The training strategy uses engine-grouped validation so observations from an unseen engine are not allowed to leak into training.

HAL / AEROSPACE CONTEXT

Engineering beyond the model

SubAero was developed for Aerothon 2026 in an aerospace challenge context associated with Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), with IIT Indore represented in the submission context.

The portfolio presents HAL here as part of the challenge and aerospace ecosystem context, not as a claim that HAL operates or deploys the SubAero software.

Physics layer

  • Isentropic compressor efficiency
  • Combustor temperature ratio
  • Turbine work coefficient
  • Thermodynamic consistency checks
  • Health bounds and EGT constraints

Validation

5-fold GroupKFold partitioning by EngineID. Deployment consistency checks target a prediction delta below 1e-6 across inference paths.
