02 / MEDIQUE · HEALTHCARE

Make waiting
intelligent.

A real-time clinic workflow platform designed to reduce uncertainty for receptionists, patients and doctors.

The problem

Clinic queues create friction when patients cannot see where they stand, reception teams cannot estimate demand and doctors receive uneven workloads.

Engineering

Built around Flutter and Firebase/Firestore real-time synchronization, with patient-facing updates, receptionist workflows, smart wait-time logic and notification-oriented interactions.

PRODUCT ENGINEERING

What makes it interesting

  • Real-time state synchronization
  • Operational workflow design
  • Wait-time intelligence
  • Patient experience
  • Cross-platform application

Hackathon constraint

The product was designed around a fast receptionist workflow, live updates and meaningful wait-time predictions rather than a static queue display.
